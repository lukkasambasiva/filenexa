About FileNexa

Welcome to FileNexa – Free Online File Tools.

FileNexa is a free online platform that provides simple and easy-to-use tools for working with PDF, Word, Excel, JPG and QR files.

What We Provide

  • JPG to PDF conversion
  • PDF to JPG conversion
  • PDF to Word conversion
  • Word to PDF conversion
  • PDF to Excel conversion
  • Excel to PDF conversion
  • PDF merging
  • PDF page numbering
  • Text to QR code generation
  • QR code scanning

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide useful file tools that are simple, convenient and accessible from different devices, including mobile phones, tablets and computers.

Free Online Tools

FileNexa is designed to make common file-related tasks easier without requiring complicated software installation.
